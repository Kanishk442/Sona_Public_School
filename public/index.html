About us

About Sona Public School

Sona Public School, Chauhan Patti, Delhi, is a caring learning environment where children are encouraged to explore, create and grow with confidence, guided by values.

Our Vision

To nurture curious, confident and compassionate learners who grow into responsible citizens.

Our Mission

To give every child a safe, joyful and inclusive place to learn, with strong academics, good values and room for creativity.
